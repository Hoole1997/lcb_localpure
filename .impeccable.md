## Design Context

### Users
Mobile music listeners who use the app to discover online tracks, manage a play queue, and stay immersed in playback with minimal interruption.

### Brand Personality
Immersive, refined, and lively. The product should feel like a focused modern record player rather than a generic utility screen.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the creator-provided Figma screens: a dark continuous canvas, restrained violet/pink atmosphere, crisp vector controls, circular record imagery, and compact readable typography. Motion should be smooth and purposeful. Avoid bulky selection fills, blurry raster icons, duplicated decoration, visual separators, and ornamental effects that compete with the music.

### Design Principles
1. Keep playback and its current state visually dominant.
2. Use progressive disclosure for secondary content such as lyrics and track descriptions.
3. Animate only transform and opacity, respect Android reduced-motion settings, and preserve 60 fps on mid-range devices.
4. Use clear semantic labels so platform descriptions are never presented as lyrics.
5. Preserve state across lifecycle changes and keep MediaSession as the single playback source of truth.
